# Planning Poker — Help Tooltips

One section per UI element. Tooltip text only, user-facing, under 150 characters.

## Mode toggle (Sprint-bound / Free)
Sprint-bound pulls tickets from a Jira sprint and writes estimates back. Free lets you add items by hand with no Jira write-back.

## Sprint selector
Pick the sprint to estimate. Defaults to the next upcoming sprint; recent sprints are also listed.

## Session title (Free mode)
Name this estimation session so the team knows what they're sizing.

## Create session
Starts the room and generates a link to share with your team. Only signed-in users can create a session.

## Join as guest
Enter a display name to join without signing in. Guests can vote but can't add items or run the session.

## Room link
Share this with your team. Anyone with the link can join while the session is active.

## Item list
Items to estimate. Green = estimated, highlighted = current, grey = skipped, plain = pending.

## Add item (Free mode)
Type a title to add something to estimate. Available to the Scrum Master only.

## Refresh tickets (Sprint-bound)
Re-pull the sprint from Jira to add tickets moved in since you started. Items you've already voted on stay put.

## Estimation cards
Pick your estimate. Choices stay hidden until everyone has voted or the SM reveals.

## "?" card
Use when you don't have enough information to estimate. It can't be chosen as the final value.

## Change vote
Switch your card any time before votes are revealed.

## Reveal votes
Show everyone's cards now, without waiting for the last voter. Scrum Master only.

## Pick final estimate
Choose the agreed value from the cards that were cast. Sprint-bound sessions write it to Jira.

## Write-back failed
Saving the estimate to Jira didn't go through. The item stays open — retry when ready. Nothing was lost.

## Skip item
Move past this item with no estimate and no Jira write-back. Scrum Master only.

## Participant list
Who's in the room. Shows a checkmark when each person has voted, then their value after reveal.

## Close session
End the session and lock it to a read-only summary. This can't be undone. Scrum Master only.

## Session summary
Final recap: items estimated, items skipped, total story points, and each item's estimate.
