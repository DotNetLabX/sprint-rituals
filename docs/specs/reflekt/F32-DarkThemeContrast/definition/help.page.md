# Dark Theme Contrast — Guide

## I'm Done Button

When you've finished adding your cards during the Writing phase, click "I'm done" to let the facilitator know you're ready to move on. You can still add or edit cards after clicking it. The facilitator uses this signal to decide when to advance to the next phase.

## Readiness Counter

The readiness counter (e.g., "2/5 done") shows how many participants have signaled they're finished writing. Everyone in the retro can see it. The facilitator uses it to decide when to advance — waiting for everyone, or nudging stragglers.

## Phase Stepper

The phase stepper at the top of the retro board shows where you are in the retro flow. Retros move through five phases in order: Writing, Grouping, Voting, Discussing, and Reporting. Only the facilitator can advance phases. The current phase is highlighted; upcoming phases appear dimmed until reached.

## Writing Phase

During Writing, every participant privately adds cards to the reflection columns. Cards are anonymous to other participants during this phase. Take your time — add what went well, what didn't, and what the team should start doing. Click "I'm done" when you've captured your thoughts.

## Grouping Phase

During Grouping, the facilitator organizes similar cards into groups. This reduces duplication and focuses the discussion. Participants can see the grouping happen in real time but only the facilitator moves cards.

## Voting Phase

During Voting, each participant gets a limited number of votes to cast on the cards or groups they think are most important to discuss. Your remaining vote budget is displayed. Vote on what matters most to you — the highest-voted items are discussed first.

## Discussing Phase

During Discussing, the facilitator selects groups to talk through with the team. The group under discussion is highlighted for everyone. Action items can be created here to capture commitments. Only the facilitator creates and edits action items.

## Reporting Phase

The Reporting phase shows a summary of the retro: which topics were discussed, how many votes each received, and all action items created. Everyone can see the report. The facilitator can export it as markdown for sharing with the wider team.
