# Dark Theme Contrast — Tooltips

## I'm Done Button

Mark yourself as finished writing. The facilitator can see your status.

## Readiness Counter

Shows how many participants have finished writing out of the total joined.

## Phase Stepper

Shows the retro phases in order. The highlighted phase is the current one.

## Writing Phase

Everyone adds cards privately. Click "I'm done" when finished.

## Grouping Phase

The facilitator groups similar cards together before voting.

## Voting Phase

Vote on the cards you want to discuss. Your vote budget is shown.

## Discussing Phase

The facilitator picks groups to discuss. Action items are created here.

## Reporting Phase

Summary of the retro — discussed topics, votes, and action items.
