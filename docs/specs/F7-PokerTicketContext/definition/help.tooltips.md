# Poker Ticket Context — Help Tooltips

One section per UI element. Tooltip text only, user-facing, under 150 characters.

## Open in Jira
Opens this ticket in Jira in a new tab — the full story, comments and attachments live there.

## Issue type
The Jira issue type of this ticket, such as Story, Task or Bug.

## Labels
The Jira labels on this ticket.

## Jira story points
The points this ticket has in Jira. Deciding an estimate here writes the new value to Jira.

## Description preview
The start of the ticket's story. Use "Read in Jira" for the full text.

## Subtasks
The pieces of work under this ticket and who has them. Click a key to open it in Jira.

## Couldn't load details from Jira
Jira didn't answer when this ticket was loaded. Refresh to try again; earlier details are kept.

## No longer in sprint
This ticket was moved out of the sprint in Jira. Its votes and estimate here are kept.

## Ticket status: Not started
No voting round is open on this ticket and it has no estimate in this session.

## Private look
You're viewing another ticket — only you. The room stays on its current ticket; your votes go there.

## Look (facilitator)
View this ticket privately without moving the room. Clicking the ticket itself moves everyone there.

## Back to current
Return to the ticket the room is on, in whatever phase it is now.

## Voting is open
The room is voting on its current ticket and is waiting for your card. Go back to vote.

## Collapse / resize list
Hide the ticket list or drag its edge to resize. Remembered in this browser.

## Filter
Untick issue types to hide them; tick labels to show only tickets with those labels. This session only. Facilitator only.

## Hidden by filter
How many tickets the facilitator's filter is hiding from the list right now.

## Next unestimated
Jump to the next visible, non-skipped ticket with no estimate here or in Jira (or reset here). Facilitator only.

## AI Estimator
The AI's estimate for this ticket, hidden until reveal. It never holds up the reveal.

## Type a number
Set any estimate of 0 or more in whole or half steps, such as 3 or 2.5. Facilitator only.

## Use AI estimate
Adopt the AI's number as the final estimate, exactly as it is. It is marked AI in the summary.

## Edit estimate
Change this ticket's final estimate. In sprint mode Jira is updated first. Facilitator only.

## Reset
Clear this ticket's votes and estimate to vote again. Jira keeps its points until a new one is decided.

## AI mark in summary
This estimate was the AI's number, adopted as-is.

## Team settings
Default filter for new sessions, and the AI estimate switch. Anyone signed in can view; only Admins can change.

## AI estimate switch
Show the AI's estimate as a voter on tickets that have one. Applies to voting rounds started after the change.
